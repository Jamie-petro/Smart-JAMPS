# Limitations — say these out loud

- **Sources checked on 31 Oct 2026 only.** Rules and grants change; every item links to the page we opened.
- **Guidance, not approval.** The tool never says "you qualify" or "you will be approved". "Appears to meet" means the data we hold matches a criterion; the agency decides.
- **One case.** Rules are written for one realistic proposal (workshop + light installation + acoustic set + drinks stall). Other event types will hit requirements we have not modelled.
- **Verified vs not.** Verified (opened today, linked): SLA short-term rental / TOL, IMDA 40-working-day AEL lead time, SPF/IMDA licence exemptions + 7-day police notification, Bishan-Toa Payoh TC void-deck booking, NParks group activity permit, URA Lively Places Fund criteria.
- **Assumptions** (page blocked or not read line by line): NParks event approval for town/regional parks (GoBusiness 403), NAC Presentation & Participation Grant criteria (page 404), WeCare Arts Fund (PDF 404), Town Council rules outside Bishan-Toa Payoh.
- **Gaps** (known need, no official source found in time): road/walkway use (LTA/URA), temporary structures & fire safety (BCA/SCDF), food licensing (SFA), ticketed-event conditions, owners of Our Tampines Hub plaza and Dakota Crescent.
- **Lead times often unknown.** SLA, Town Councils and NParks do not state processing times on the pages we read — shown as "unknown — check".
- **Spaces.** Coordinates come from OneMap search; area and capacity are team estimates; land owner is our reading, not an SLA land query.
- **Renders** are AI-generated concepts, not designs, and do not show what an agency would approve.
- **AI drafting** (optional toggle) adds guidelines marked `assumption`; it is told not to invent regulations, but output is not checked line by line.
